FUNDAMENTALS OF
THE MAT. SYSTEM

  • 22 hours per day – is the time for which each aligner must be worn per day
  • 14 days – is the time for which each aligner must be word
  • All instructions are provided by MAT. (attachments plan + IPR) and must be followed
  • MAT. aligners extend approx. 1-3 mm above the gingiva for more stability as well as evenly distributed pressure on teeth and roots
  • Each aligner treatment has several phases: a treatment phase, (if necessary) a fine-tuning phase, a retention phase
  • You get an all-inclusive price which includes all phases of the treatment – incl. the first retention aligner!

MAT. medical aligner treatments are only available through dentists or orthodontists. We strongly believe that only a trained practitioner can guarantee a medically correct treatment with multiple check-ups.

FAQ

Do you accept intraoral-scans?

Yes! We accept intraoral-scans of any scanner that support stl. format or ply. format. You also have the option of providing us with your scans via an external scanning system (such as 3shape).
You don´t have a scanner yet? Just ask us about the option to buy one from us for great conditions.

Do you accept impressions or models?

No, but we accept intraoral-scans of any scanner that supports stl. format or ply. format. You also have the option of providing us with your scans via an external scanning system (such as 3shape).
We believe that precision is a must when providing medical treatments. This is best achieved through scans. You don´t have a scanner yet? Just ask us about the option to buy one from us for great conditions.

Do I have to pay for the 3D simulation?

No! The 3D simulation is always free of charge, regardless of whether the patient decides to undergo treatment with you or not. This allows you to make MAT. treatment appealing to all of your patients without any cost risk. It is even possible to request changes to each simulation. Every revision is also free of charge!

What is the cost for the retention aligner?

We at MAT. know how important it is for satisfied patients and your reputation, to make sure the new smiles last. With us, you get a retention aligner automatically and for FREE with every treatment order.
