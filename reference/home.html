WHY CHOOSE MAT.?

12 of the endless compelling reasons

01.

Clear pricing – no surprises

The treatment price is based solely on whether it’s for one or two arches. The number of aligners required doesn’t affect the cost. This means you can provide your patients with an immediate, fixed price.

02.

Advanced 3D simulation tailored to each treatment

You can present your patient with a 3D simulation of the final result. Simply share the provided file to easily showcase the simulation.

03.

3D simulation provided free of charge

No hidden cancellation fees—no obligations. The 3D simulation is completely free. This means you can proactively approach all your patients and offer them a 3D simulation.

04.

Every plan created by qualified experts

Unlike many providers, we work exclusively with qualified dental technicians and dentists to create every treatment plan.

05.

We can cover more Indications

With our system we can cover more indications than many other systems. We correct from 3 to 3, 5 to 5 or even 7 to 7.

06.

Free certification courses

We offer free, regular certification courses for MAT. users. These online trainings are led by our experienced orthodontic consultants.

07.

Data protection

All patient data is protected under GDPR. Data processing agreements are available when needed.

08.

Clear and simple instructions

IPR and attachment placement can be performed easily, following our clear instructions.

09.

Fine-tuning phase

All aligners needed for the fine-tuning phase are included free of charge until the planned 3D simulation results are achieved.

10.

Treatment ready in minimum time

Fast processing: treatment planning in 5 working days, production in just 10 days after plan approval.

11.

Optimized Chair time

With our optimised system, in total you need just 1 hour 20 minutes per patient for the entire treatment, without compromising quality.

12.

Free Aligners in case of loss

The fixed price does not only include any necessary extra aligners and retention aligners but even replacement aligners in case of loss.
